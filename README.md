# Ngaji Calvince Otieno

**Data Scientist | Biostatistics PhD Candidate, Moi University | Food Security, Geospatial AI and African NLP**

Nairobi, Kenya

I apply statistics and machine learning to food security, agriculture, health and science policy in Africa. My PhD research at Moi University, supported by a DAAD scholarship, develops multimodal Transformer-based models for early forecasting of acute food insecurity in Kenya's arid and semi-arid lands.

### Research interests
- Food security early warning and forecasting
- Geospatial analysis and remote sensing for agriculture
- Machine learning for health and social data
- Natural language processing for African languages
- Evidence for science, technology and innovation policy

### Tools
Python, R, SQL, QGIS, Tableau, SPSS, KoboToolbox
